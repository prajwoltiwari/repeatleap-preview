Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

RepeatLeap ("we", "us") is operated by North Star Ecommerce LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company with its principal office at 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit repeatleap.com, book a call or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you book a call or email us, such as your name, work email, store URL, revenue range and anything else you write.
  • Scheduling details when you book through our calendar tool.
  • Basic, aggregated website usage such as pages visited, referring site, browser and country. Our analytics does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To prepare for and run your audit call and reply to your messages.
  • To send you a proposal or follow-up about our services. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Client account data

When a client gives us access to Klaviyo, Shopify or another platform, we use that access only to deliver the agreed work. We do not sell, rent or reuse client customer data, and we remove our access when the engagement ends.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Google Workspace, for email and documents.
  • Calendly, for scheduling calls.
  • Cloudflare, for website hosting, security and cookieless analytics.
  • Members of our delivery team working on your account, who are bound to keep client information confidential.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

International transfers

We are a US company and our team works remotely from several countries. Your information may be processed outside the country where you live, with appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and client records for as long as we need them to provide our services and meet legal obligations, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, including the EU, the UK and California, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA. Email hello@repeatleap.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our analytics is cookieless. The Calendly booking calendar on our homepage may set cookies that it needs to schedule your call.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
